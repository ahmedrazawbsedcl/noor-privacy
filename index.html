Welcome to NOOR — Stories of Islam ("NOOR", "the App", "we", "us", or "our").

NOOR is an Islamic learning and storytelling application designed to provide access to Islamic stories, Qur'an, Hadith, worship learning, Islamic history, Sacred Journey experiences and Auliya-e-Hind content.

Our privacy approach: NOOR is designed with an offline-first and privacy-conscious architecture. The core application does not require users to create an account or provide personal information.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information

NOOR does not require users to create an account to use its core features.

NOOR does not intentionally collect personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Postal address
  • Account passwords or credentials
  • Contacts
  • Photos or personal documents
  • Religious profile or religious affiliation

You can use the core features of NOOR without creating an account.

2. Location Information

NOOR does not collect or store precise location information as part of its core learning experience.

If a future feature requires access to location information, the application will request the relevant permission and explain the purpose of that access before using it.

3. Learning Progress and App Preferences

NOOR may store certain application information locally on your device in order to provide its learning and personalization features.

This may include:

  • Completed stories
  • Learning progress
  • Saved or bookmarked content
  • Continue-reading information
  • Daily Noor preferences
  • Notification preferences
  • Review-prompt eligibility information
  • Application settings and preferences

This information is used to provide features such as My Learning, Continue Journey, Daily Noor and local learning progress.

This locally stored information is not intended to identify you personally and is not uploaded to a NOOR server as part of the core application.

4. Notifications

NOOR may provide optional local notifications, including Daily Noor learning reminders.

Local notifications are scheduled and delivered by your device. The core Daily Noor reminder functionality does not require a remote push-notification account.

You can control or disable notifications through the application's settings and/or your device settings.

5. Qur'an and Audio Content

NOOR provides Qur'an-related content and audio experiences.

Certain audio content may be accessed from external audio providers or services when an internet connection is available.

Where supported, audio may be temporarily cached or stored locally on your device to improve playback and offline usability.

NOOR does not use Qur'an reading or listening activity to create a personal religious profile.

6. External Services and Links

Certain optional features may direct you to external services, websites or content providers.

These may include:

  • Google Play
  • YouTube
  • External audio providers
  • Other content services used by optional features

When you leave NOOR or interact directly with an external service, that service may collect or process information according to its own privacy policy and terms.

NOOR does not control the privacy practices of third-party services.

7. YouTube Videos

NOOR may provide optional links to YouTube videos associated with stories.

For example, a story may include an option such as "Watch on YouTube".

Selecting such an option may open YouTube or a YouTube-supported viewing experience.

Your interaction with YouTube is governed by Google's and YouTube's applicable privacy policies and terms.

NOOR does not receive your YouTube account credentials merely because you view a linked video.

8. Advertising

Initial Release:

The initial release of NOOR is intended to be provided without advertising.

NOOR does not intentionally use advertising identifiers or advertising SDKs for the purpose of displaying advertisements in the initial release.

If advertising is introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy and the applicable Google Play Data Safety information will be updated to accurately describe the relevant data practices before or when the functionality is introduced.

9. Analytics and Tracking

NOOR does not intentionally use third-party analytics services for the core application unless such a service is specifically introduced and disclosed in an updated version of this Privacy Policy.

NOOR is designed to minimize unnecessary tracking.

NOOR does not intentionally use learning activity to create a personal religious profile.

10. Google Play In-App Review

NOOR may use the Google Play In-App Review mechanism to provide eligible users with an opportunity to rate or review the application.

The review request is designed to be occasional and non-intrusive.

NOOR does not ask users to provide a particular rating and does not provide rewards in exchange for a positive rating.

NOOR does not collect or store your Google Play rating, Google account credentials, or the contents of your Google Play review.

11. Children's Privacy

NOOR is designed as a For All Ages learning experience.

NOOR does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to access its core educational features.

The application does not intentionally request children's personal information for advertising, profiling or unrelated purposes.

NOOR will continue to follow applicable Google Play requirements concerning children and audiences.

12. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application.

Because much of NOOR's learning progress and preferences are stored locally on the user's device, the security of that information also depends on the security of the device itself.

Users should keep their device operating system and security mechanisms up to date.

13. Data Retention

NOOR does not maintain a central personal user profile for users of the core application.

Information stored locally by NOOR remains on the user's device until it is removed by the user, the application, or the operating system.

For example, local application data may be removed when the user clears the application's data or uninstalls the application.

If future versions introduce account-based services or server-side personal data, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain the associated retention practices.

14. Data Deletion

The initial NOOR experience does not require a user account and therefore does not maintain a central NOOR account containing personal information.

Users can remove locally stored application data using applicable device settings or by uninstalling the application.

If future versions introduce account creation or server-side personal information, NOOR will provide an appropriate mechanism for data deletion and will update this Privacy Policy accordingly.

15. Third-Party Services

NOOR may use third-party technologies or services to provide specific optional functionality.

Any third-party service included in a future release will be reviewed for its applicable privacy and data practices.

Where a third-party service collects information, the applicable third party's privacy policy may also apply.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when NOOR's features, services or data practices change.

When significant changes are made, the updated policy will be published at the official Privacy Policy URL.

The Last Updated date at the beginning of this policy will also be updated.
